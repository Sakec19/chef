Install on iPhone

Open this page in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. After the first visit, the app works offline from the home screen. See INSTALL-IPHONE.md in the project folder for steps.

Note: plans saved in Safari do not automatically carry over to the home-screen app — use Backup (JSON) or Save offline file to move them. That is an iOS storage behaviour, not a bug in this app.

About

Fridge Chef · offline PWA · v1.3.0
Hybrid AI (v1.3.0): 48 built-in offline recipes always work; optional AI (Custom proxy / Vercel recommended, or direct provider key) pulls NEW recipes when online, validates + diet-checks them, and caches them for offline reuse. Dark mode (System / Light / Dark), recipes for chosen days, and ingredients as a preference (not a restriction) with Also needs shown. Diet stays a strict rule. All prices, calories and nutrients are estimates. Recipes are checked against your diet and pantry quantities locally. No sign-in, no account, no data leaves this device unless you share or export it — and your API key is never included in backups.
